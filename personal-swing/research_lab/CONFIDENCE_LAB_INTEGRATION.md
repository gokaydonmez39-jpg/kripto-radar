## What is implemented
1. Explicit target-before-stop-by-15-session binary estimand; TARGET=1, STOP or TIMEOUT=0; TIMEOUT is not necessarily a monetary loss. Gross theoretical R minus explicit cost, with capped 2R target and gap-aware stop <= -1R. Ambiguous dual-touch, corporate action and unavailable labels are censored, not zero returns or failed forecasts. Native bracket witness required; Kronos close-only cannot determine bracket contact.
2. Brier and constant-baseline Brier/skill, fixed five calibration bins, descriptive Wilson interval, overlap-cluster count and outcome counts. No inferred probabilities from technical scores, no invented empirical confidence. Caller-provided probabilities/baseline are NOT validated model predictions; status CALLER_WITNESS_NOT_ATTESTED, decision_influence NO_GO always. 30 clusters is a conservative screening threshold only, not proof of independence or power.
3. Forward-only purged split with >=15 XNAS-session embargo and pre-origin availability check. Session ordinals must come from independently verified XNAS calendar; not a calendar-day approximation. Train only before test and label maturity before boundary; no post-test training.
4. Pearson concentration diagnostics for explicitly prealigned same-session returns; alignment not attested, no PCA fit, no portfolio weight or Kelly output.
